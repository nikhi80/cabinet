# Cabinet Terms

Cabinet is a personal organization tool for information you enter. It is not a
medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment,
prescription guidance, or vaccination recommendations. Ask a qualified
healthcare professional about medical decisions and verify recorded dates
against official records.

You are responsible for the accuracy of information you add and for keeping
exports and device access secure. Do not use Cabinet as the only copy of
important medical records. The current local-device flow does not offer Firebase
account creation, sign-in, or account deletion. Removing local data does not
delete copies you exported or copied elsewhere.

Firebase authentication code remains in the project but is not part of the
current app flow. Cabinet does not provide cloud synchronization in this
release. This starter agreement should be reviewed and adapted before public
distribution.
